## Sprout Siege 2.2: Zombie Tricks & the Mushroom Grove

Plant a garden and hold off waves of cartoon zombies before they reach the greenhouse. Free, on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and the web.

### New in 2.2
- **Zombies now have their own tricks**, not just their own looks:
  - **Jogger** dodges into the next lane the first time a plant blocks it.
  - **Barrel**: when the barrel breaks, it rolls ahead and smashes into your first plant.
  - **Brute** stomps the ground, making nearby plants too dizzy to fight for a moment.
  - **Pumpkin-head** spits pumpkin seeds at plants ahead.
  - **Parka** throws snowballs that freeze a plant in a block of ice.
  - **Scarecrow** burns twice as easily, and when its hat falls off a crow flies out to steal your dew. Tap the crow to scare it away!
- **3 new zombies:** Gnome (comes in threes), Wheelbarrow (buries the first plant it reaches, then hurries on) and Beekeeper (its bees sting nearby plants).
- **Boss: the Compost Giant** joins the final horde of every world. It calls in helper zombies, stomps your plants and has its own health bar.
- **4 new plants:** Moon Mushroom (cheap dew), Popcorn Pod (kernels bounce between zombies), Bramble Hedge (tough, pricks anything that bites it) and Sprinkler Bloom (splashes a 3x3 area). That makes 18 plants.
- **World 6: Mushroom Grove**, with 10 new levels, glowing toadstools, its own music, and rain and thunder. That makes 60 levels.
- **Weather:** rainy levels drop more dew but make the lanes muddy; on windy levels gusts blow the zombies toward you.
- **Stars:** earn up to 3 stars on every level (win, use no zappers, lose 2 plants or fewer). Your stars show on the map.
- **Daily Challenge:** a new level every day, with its own plants and a twist like Speedy Zombies, Dry Spell or Giant Finale. Opens after Level 1-3.
- New results screen, new How to Play pages, and a bigger Field Guide.
- Save codes and save files now carry your stars too. Older codes still work.

### Downloads

| Platform | File | How to install |
|---|---|---|
| **iPhone / iPad** | No download needed | Open **https://sadirhossain0.github.io/sprout-siege/** in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and open it once while online. It then works offline, fullscreen. Already installed? Open it while online and it updates itself. |
| **Android** (5.0+) | `SproutSiege-2.2-Android.apk` | Open it on your phone and allow "Install unknown apps". If Play Protect warns, tap **More details → Install anyway**. Installs over older versions and keeps your progress. |
| **Windows** 10/11 | `SproutSiege-2.2-Windows.zip` | Extract, open the `Sprout Siege` folder, run **Sprout Siege.exe**. If SmartScreen appears: **More info → Run anyway**. |
| **Mac** (M1–M4) | `SproutSiege-2.2-Mac-AppleSilicon.zip` | Unzip, drag **Sprout Siege** into Applications, open it. The first time: **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway**. |
| **Mac** (Intel) | `SproutSiege-2.2-Mac-Intel.zip` | Same as above. |
| **Any browser** | `SproutSiege-2.2-Web.zip` | The web app files, for hosting anywhere. Or just play at the link above. |

The security warnings appear because the apps aren't signed with paid Google, Microsoft or Apple developer certificates.
