## Sprout Siege 2.1: Save Codes & Backups

Plant a garden and hold off waves of cartoon zombies before they reach the greenhouse. Free, on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and the web.

### New in 2.1
- **Save codes:** copy a short code (like `SPRT-N.18ZDZ.V.EB.1-GCBO`) on one device and enter it on another to bring your progress along. No account needed.
- **Save files:** export your progress as a backup file and import it on any device. On Android the file goes straight to your Downloads folder.
- Find both in **Options → Save Codes & Backups**. Loading a code or file only ever adds progress, so you can't lose levels by loading an older save.

### Also in 2.0
- **12 kinds of cartoon garden zombies**, each with something growing on its head: Farmhand, Lid, Jogger, Barrel, Acrobat, Brute, Scarecrow, Pumpkin-head, Gardener (heals its friends), Mailman (drops bonus dew), Parka (can't be slowed) and Skater (fast until hit).
- **5 worlds, 50 levels:** Backyard, Autumn Orchard, Moonlit Garden, Frosty Garden and Sunny Beach, each with its own scenery, music and twist.
- **14 plants**, including Thorn Rose, Ivy Snare, Breeze Bloom and Chestnut Lobber, plus a seed picker once you own more than 10.
- Guided tutorial (with a Skip button), How to Play book, Watch Demo mode and Field Guide.
